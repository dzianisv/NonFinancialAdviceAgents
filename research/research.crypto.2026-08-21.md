## The key finding: denominate fundamentals in native units

USD-denominated fundamentals during a 20–30% price move are circular — they measure the same move twice. Stripping out price mark-up:

| Metric | USD growth | Price growth | **Native-unit growth** | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ETH fee revenue | $1.03M → $2.93M (+184%) | +29.9% | **+119% in ETH terms** | Real activity growth. Non-circular. **Survives** |
| SOL fee revenue | $3.35M → $4.70M (+40%) | +19.4% | +17% in SOL terms | Weakly positive |
| SOL TVL | $4.78B → $5.39B (+12.8%) | +19.4% | **−7% in SOL terms** | Capital **left** Solana. Pure mark-up. **Fails** |

The headline "SOL TVL +12.8%" is not a bull signal — TVL grew *slower* than price, meaning native-denominated capital shrank. ETH is ranked first because its fee growth survives the circularity test, **not** because it printed the biggest number (that would just be beta).
